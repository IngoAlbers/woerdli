How to play

Guess the Swiss German word in 6 tries. Every word has 5 letters, and Ä, Ö and Ü each count as one letter with their own key.

Swiss German has no official spelling, so any 5 letters count as a guess. The answers use common Zürich-style spellings, like CHIND, LUEGE and ÖPPIS. A new word comes out every day at midnight.
